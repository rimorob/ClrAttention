## 1. Hypotheses (fixed now)

- **H1 (primary, Arm A only).** tcaCLR's pooled co-membership AUPR exceeds aCLR's on the Complex Portal human complexes in HepG2 pseudobulk. This is a fixed-truth estimand; the complex-level interval (section 5) shows why it does not extend to "protein complexes" in general.
- **H2 (size prediction, descriptive, no p-value anywhere).** The tcaCLR minus aCLR difference is expected to be positive in complexes of 20 or more genes pooled, and not positive in complexes of fewer than 20 genes (the net4 data were near zero there, -0.003 and -0.004; the simulation was markedly negative at 5 to 10 genes, -0.26 to -0.53, so it may be markedly negative). No monotone dependence on size above 20 and no advantage over CLR in the largest complexes is predicted (the simulation was seed-dependent at 70 to 100 genes). Labels: "positive" = the lower bound of the gene-plus-experiment interval exceeds 0; "not positive" = d at most 0.01 (a result between 0 and 0.01 with an interval including 0 is reported as "near zero"). A script writes "the prediction held / partly held / did not hold". The bins are the net4 bins, not the theory's: the theory memo puts the forced-leakage boundary at n below keff, about 10, so the 7 to 19 bin straddles it; 7 to 10 and 11 to 19 are reported separately and descriptively. The slope of the mean within-complex score on log complex size, per method, is reported as the continuous form. H2 was derived after the net4 look, and the theory memo that states it had seen the net4 strata, so it is not an independent theoretical prediction. **Simulation outcome (17 Nov; `docs/plans/sim_planted_results.md`, rules fixed before running):** the planted-module simulation gives a lower edge of 20 genes for the "positive" class at each of N = 150, 400 and 800 (the rule: smallest module size at which tcaCLR minus aCLR is strictly positive in at least 9 of 10 seeds, Gaussian arm, identity marginals), which agrees with the net4 bin edge registered here; tcaCLR beat aCLR in 10 of 10 seeds at sizes 20 and 40 and in 0 of 10 at sizes 5 and 10. It was not reliably positive at sizes 70 and 100 (for example 7 to 8 of 10 at N = 800 and 3 of 10 at size 100 at N = 400), and tcaCLR lost to CLR at those sizes and collapsed on strong large modules, so H2's "positive at 20 or more" is expected to hold for the 20 to 40 range and is not guaranteed for the very large complexes. The simulation does not support a size-invariance mechanism for tcaCLR (its slope in module size was far steeper than the 0.3 z per nat predicted); the plan therefore makes no mechanism claim.
- **H3 (comparators).** Per arm, tcaCLR minus each of aCLR, CLR, |r|, GENIE3, PIDC and GRNBoost2 (the same six in both arms). "tcaCLR is the best of the [k] methods run" is claimed only if every one of the comparisons is won at one-sided 0.025 (intersection-union, no correction). Any individual comparator statement uses Holm over the comparisons run at 0.025. Trap, stated in advance: tcaCLR minus CLR has about half the standard error of tcaCLR minus aCLR on *E. coli* (k 0.016 to 0.027 against 0.037 to 0.041), so it "excludes 0" more easily. If a comparator is not run, the statement is "best of the [k] methods run". |r| was first on *E. coli* complexes (29.2) and ahead of CLR and aCLR on net4, so the statement may well fail, and the report will say so.

## 2. Arms

**Arm A (primary): human HepG2 Perturb-seq pseudobulk with EBI Complex Portal human (taxon 9606).**
- Data: GEO GSE264667, HepG2 h5ad (5.6 GB), not yet downloaded. Pseudobulk recipe: `analysis/perturbseq_bulk.R` as run for K562, byte-identical; any forced deviation is decided on a label-free criterion and logged before any score. The operator runs on all measured genes; pairs are scored among truth genes (the net4 convention). Fallback if HepG2 fails the data gates: Jurkat (9.4 GB), same truth.
- Truth: Complex Portal 9606 (downloaded, 5.4 MB), never scored. On a proxy universe (K562 gene names): 329 complexes, n_eff 31, 60% of positive pairs are CORUM positives and 40% are not. Both are recomputed on the real HepG2 universe before any score. Complex Portal human holds the 40S and 60S subunits as separate complexes, so cross-subunit pairs are negatives, the opposite of the K562 CORUM truth on which tcaCLR was selected; this is handled by the sensitivities in section 5.
- **Independence is limited.** New cell type and new truth set, but the same laboratory, CRISPRi essential-gene library family and pipeline as K562 and RPE1, and 60% of positives shared with CORUM. The claim is therefore cross-cell-type and cross-truth transfer within one lineage, not cross-technology transfer.
- **Built-in data-truth link.** The library perturbs essential genes, including ribosomal, proteasomal and spliceosomal subunits that are truth members; knocking down one subunit perturbs the others, so co-membership is partly built into the screen design, as for Kemmeren. [N] of the [M] truth genes are themselves perturbed in the panel (counted label-free before any score and printed with the result).
- Power on the real HepG2 universe (n_eff 31.69; freeze record section 3): at +0.03 the power is 0.98 gene-only and 0.86 with the net4-sized experiment component (rho 0.893); at +0.05, adequate in both. (The earlier figures 0.80 and 0.56 were the n_eff 15.6 row, not n_eff 31; corrected.) The registered gate n_eff of at least 27 is met. The planning effect is anchored on RPE1 merged CORUM (+0.036), the only same-lineage transfer; K562 (+0.059) is in-sample and net4 (+0.096) is another organism.

**Arm B (secondary estimate): yeast Kemmeren deletion compendium with CYC2008. Data independent of net4; truth largely shared with the consumed Complex Portal yeast.**
- Data: GEO superseries GSE42528 (GSE42527, 700 responsive mutants, and GSE42526, 784 non-responsive; GPL11232), already in `data/yeast`: 6,169 ORFs by 1,283 profiles (the full matrix; the 652-profile responsive-only file is not used, chosen now so the choice cannot depend on a result).
- Truth: CYC2008 (408 complexes, 1,627 ORFs; from an Internet Archive capture of 16 Feb 2020 because the authors' server did not answer). The PI assumes academic research use is permitted; the licence is not independently verified, and if it cannot be resolved before freezing, Arm B is run for the project record and may be unpublishable. On the Kemmeren universe: 213 merged complexes, 10,308 positive pairs, n_eff 10.0; 89.5% of positive pairs are also Complex Portal yeast positives (the 1,079 novel pairs have n_eff about 7 and are reported descriptively).
- Known caveats: gate F of an earlier reciprocity preregistration failed on this matrix (median reproducibility 0.122 for mutants against 0.296 for wild types); 376 of the 1,600 truth genes are themselves deleted in the panel; the hs1991 hybridisation-orientation question.
- Reported with the same estimand, intervals and the net4 fixed sentences; **no p-value, no claim rule.** Power (n_eff 10): 0.63 (gene-only) or 0.40 (rho 0.89) for +0.03; 0.97 or 0.81 for +0.05; the 20-or-more stratum has n_eff 4 to 8 and its interval is too wide to read.
- Comparators in Arm B: aCLR, CLR, |r|, PIDC, GENIE3 and GRNBoost2, so that both arms have a complete comparison table (the PI's requirement, 17 Nov). Arm B still carries no p-value and no claim rule.

**Claim rule (Arm A, if (W))**: "tcaCLR exceeds aCLR in pooled co-membership AUPR on the Complex Portal human complexes in HepG2 Perturb-seq pseudobulk (same laboratory and screen family as the discovery data; cross-cell-type and cross-truth transfer, not cross-technology)", accompanied by the *E. coli* failure and by the descriptors of section 5. If more than 80% of d is carried by complexes of 20 or more genes the headline adds: "the gain is carried by complexes of 20 or more genes." Arm B does not enter the claim; if it disagrees, its interval is reported next to the claim.

## 3. Methods (all fixed; nothing tuned)

- aCLR, tcaCLR: the exact R implementation and settings used on K562 and net4 (B-spline MI, row z-score clipped at 0, Stouffer, softmax over the top 50 with effective partners 10, lazy walk P = 0.5 I + 0.5 A, t = 9, symmetric read-out; tcaCLR adds log P^9 with unreachable pairs NA, row statistics over reachable pairs, second CLR clipped at 0, Stouffer). Code commit and operator hash are recorded in the freeze file.
- CLR: Stouffer version. |r|: absolute Pearson correlation.
- GENIE3 (both arms): all genes as regulators, random forest, K = sqrt, 1,000 trees, `set.seed(20261016)`, `doRNG`. PIDC: `tools/pidc_chunked.jl` (bit-identical to the package). GRNBoost2 (both arms): `tools/grnboost2.py`, legacy early stopping.
- Equivalence gates as in the scoreboard competitor study (re-implementations match their references on a 300-to-600-gene subset before any full run).
- No method parameter is changed for any arm; t = 9 is the label-free choice already made.
- **Exploratory read-outs of the same operator**, one table, intervals only, none may be cited in place of H1, nothing is selected among them: aCLR within-row percentile (rank read-out); tcaCLR at t = 1 (equals CLR re-standardised within candidate sets); CLR on raw P^9 (linear domain); fixed-exponent inflation of P^9 with r in {1.5, 2}, row z; degree-preserving rewiring of A (10 draws) then walk then tcaCLR; Sinkhorn-balanced A then walk then tcaCLR; hub-stratified evaluation (MI row mean); tcaCLR with row statistics over all entries (floor log 1e-300, the non-robust form), which the simulation showed restores closed modules but ranks them mostly by reachability. Matched-depth aCLR is not run (both methods are at t = 9 and the stacking study showed depth is not the lever).

The human truth is built by `analysis/complex_confirm/build_yeast_complex_truth.R` with no rule change (merging, paralog and cross-subunit exclusions as on net4). The ribosome list uses the same rule (30% or more r-protein genes by the label-free regex `^RP[LS][0-9]+[A-Z]?$`). No human-specific rule is introduced (the drafter has seen which net4 complexes carried the effect).

## 5. Estimand, inference and reporting (fixed before any score)

- **Estimand (Arm A):** d = AUPR(tcaCLR) minus AUPR(aCLR), pooled over all pairs among the genes of the frozen Complex Portal human truth, t = 9, raw scores, `ap_fast` tie convention. Fixed-truth estimand.
- **Primary SE = sqrt(SE_gene^2 + SE_exp^2).** SE_gene: paired delete-half jackknife over the truth's genes, 500 replicates, `set.seed(<fixed in the freeze>)`, halves drawn for all truths in table order. SE_exp: paired delete-half jackknife over perturbations, 100 replicates, seeds fixed in the freeze, CLR, aCLR and tcaCLR refitted per replicate. One-sided p < 0.025 against 0 and d at least +0.005. This is the net4 convention. The complex-level delete-half interval (500 replicates, seed fixed) is a sensitivity and never decides; on net4 it was 5.6 times the gene-level standard error and its interval included 0, so it will probably include 0 here, which is why the claim is fixed-truth.
- **Mandatory descriptors printed in the same row** (computed by the script before anyone reads them): the complex-level interval; the share of d carried by the three complexes with the largest per-complex contribution; the number and fraction of complexes with 10 or more positives whose per-complex AP improves; replicate-distribution skewness and the percentile interval; and, per complex size class, the reachable-entry count and the in-module share of reachable entries for member rows (the simulation showed that tcaCLR collapses when a module's attention is closed, so the reachable set is the module plus 2 to 3 genes and the reachable-only row z-score has no background; the global reachability gate cannot see this because it is a per-row failure, so these numbers are reported with every result and any size class with a median reachable count under 1.5 times its size is flagged).
- **Key secondary (breadth):** one-sided sign test (tcaCLR > aCLR) over complexes with 10 or more positives at 0.025; it does not gate H1. Fixed sentences: pass: "tcaCLR improved the median complex"; fail: "the pooled gain is carried by [k] complexes; tcaCLR did not improve the median complex."
- **Further registered secondaries** (intervals and script-chosen sentences, no p-values): `noribo`; `large` and `small`; `xsub_excl` and `ribo_joined`; a split of the pair universe into pairs where neither gene is perturbed in the panel versus the rest (if the gain is confined to pairs involving perturbed genes it is a property of the screen design and the paper says so).
- The pooled AUPR stays primary and the ribosome is not excluded or down-weighted in it: the mechanism predicts the large-module gain, and a ribosome-excluded primary would be a post-net4 choice with no precedent.
- **Experiment component for comparators that cannot be refitted.** SE_exp requires refitting the methods on each half of the experiments. That is feasible for CLR, aCLR, tcaCLR and |r| (seconds to minutes each) and not for GENIE3, GRNBoost2 and PIDC (GENIE3 and GRNBoost2: hours per fit, so 100 replicates are about 700 and 370 hours; PIDC: one refit takes about 11 to 18 minutes on Arm A and 5 to 8 on Arm B, so 100 refits are about 19 to 30 and 8 to 13 hours, measured by the build stage, replacing the earlier "seconds to minutes"). For H3 comparisons against GENIE3, GRNBoost2 and PIDC the SE is therefore SE_gene only, as in the scoreboard, and every sentence that uses those two comparisons states that the experiment component is not included and the interval understates the total uncertainty. The "best of the [k] methods run" statement carries the same sentence. PI decision, 17 Nov: no experiment-level resampling for these two ("no need for a full resampling, just note that error bars are narrower than they should be"); the sentence is therefore a required note in every table, figure caption and sentence that shows or uses their intervals: "error bars for GENIE3, GRNBoost2 and PIDC comparisons include gene resampling only and are narrower than they should be." (PIDC was added on 17 Nov, by the lead, on the same ground as the PI's decision for the other two: its refits cost 8 to 30 hours per arm.)
- Arm B: the same estimand and descriptors with SE_exp over mutants (100 delete-half replicates; the experiment-to-gene ratio is unknown, so power is shown for 0, 0.89 and 1.78), no p-value.
- Pairwise differences among all methods run are reported as estimates with total intervals; no unadjusted p-values other than H1 and the families in H3.

## 6. Gates (in order; each writes a CSV; any failure stops the job before any truth is read)

1. Data gates, label-free: gene universe and profile counts match the frozen build; matrix hash matches; no duplicated gene names (as for RPE1, where HSPA14 was dropped by a rule fixed beforehand); the HepG2 recipe is byte-identical to K562's or each deviation is logged.
2. Truth gates: n_eff and complex counts match the freeze. Arm A: n_eff at least 27 on the real universe for the registered sensitivity of +0.03; between 15 and 27 the test runs with its sensitivity stated as +0.04; below 15, estimate only and the arm is not a test.
3. Operator gates, label-free, on both matrices: implementation gate (tcaCLR at t = 1 equals CLR re-standardised within candidate sets, tolerance 1e-10); reachability diagnostic (median row SD of log P^9 over reachable entries at least 1 nat and unreachable share at most 5%; otherwise the log read-out is degenerate and the arm is "not evaluable").
4. Method gates: equivalence checks of section 3; deterministic seeds; peak memory logged.
5. Score gates: AUPR reproduces on a fixed smoke subset to 1e-12 against the registered reference.

## 7. Compute and scheduling

Everything runs through the resource-aware scheduler at 70 to 80% utilisation, one run, no retries beyond the automatic out-of-memory retry. ## 8. Disclosure and what is not claimed

- Drafter's exposure: the drafter knows the net4 and *E. coli* estimates, the net4 size strata and the net4 per-complex table (which complexes carried the effect), the *E. coli* per-complex results, and the theory memo, which was written with the net4 strata in view; H2's thresholds come from that exposure. Structure counts only have been seen for CYC2008, Complex Portal human (on a K562 proxy universe) and Kemmeren; the Kemmeren expression was opened label-free earlier (reproducibility, row names). No method has been scored on any of them.
- Not claimed, whatever the result: that tcaCLR is a theoretically justified or optimal estimator (the theory review calls it a heuristic with derivable properties); a replication of the *E. coli* test; cross-technology transfer; generalisation to complexes outside the truth; any statement about the mechanism (the exploratory table is descriptive); any statement about direct regulatory edges; any statement about a method on a dataset where it was not run; p-values for H2 anywhere.
- Limitations stated in any report: shared laboratory and pipeline in Arm A; the built-in data-truth link in both arms; reused truth in Arm B; the CYC2008 licence; the Kemmeren reproducibility caveat; a K562-selected method.
