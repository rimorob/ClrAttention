*Written while applying `docs/plans/grnboost2_rerun_change_inventory.md`. Each entry is a sentence where the text says what a GRNBoost2 result counts as or means, not just what the number is. Sources: `results/macro_aupr_v3/macro_vs_attention.csv`, `results/macro_aupr_v3/macro_strata.csv`, `results/beeline_baselines/summary.csv`, `results/baselines_*/metrics.csv`, `results/sota_table_gb2_legacy.md`.    - Old: "Its mean average precision is higher than that of |r| (3 of 4 cells) and of our GRNBoost2 runs (4 of 4; not interpreted until the audit of our GRNBoost2 wrapper, Methods), and higher than PIDC's, Holm-significantly only on EcoMAC at all evidence levels (mean difference −0.017, negative favouring attention);"
   - New: "... higher than that of |r| (3 of 4 cells), of GRNBoost2 (1 of 4 cells, EcoMAC at SC evidence, Holm P = 0.004; the sign favours attention in 4 of 4 cells and both intervals exclude 0 in 4 of 4, but the Holm-adjusted P of the other three cells is 0.0916, 0.184 and 0.0916; Supplementary Table 9) and higher than PIDC's, Holm-significantly only on EcoMAC at all evidence levels (...)"
   - Why: the count under the paper's rule falls from 4 of 4 to 1 of 4. The sentence now reports a "higher" claim in one sentence for comparators with different counts; a reviewer should decide whether "higher than ... GRNBoost2" may stay as a claim for the 3 cells where the Holm P is above 0.05, and whether the whole family-of-40 interpretation is unchanged now that GRNBoost2 inputs were replaced after the first run (Holm step-down moved other comparators' P values; no decision changed).

2. `docs/whitepapers/paper.md:126` and `docs/whitepapers/supplement.md:304` (Reading 3): "attention's win rate is highest (60–90%) in regulons of 100 genes or more".
   - New: "(60–100%)". Source: `macro_strata.csv`, size >= 100 stratum, all comparators and the four E. coli cells: attention win share 60% to 100% (GRNBoost2: 100%, 88%, 90%, 88%; n = 10, 17, 10, 16; the 100% cell is M3D SC, n = 10). Whether "5–24 genes" still describes the pooled picture across comparators was not recomputed. A reviewer should say whether the sentence needs restating per comparator. The interpretation sentence after it ("The pooled gain therefore comes from ranking pairs across regulons, not from better ranking within each regulon") was not touched.

3. `docs/whitepapers/paper.md:154` (BEELINE).
   - Old: "our GRNBoost2 runs score below it in 7 of 7 datasets (−0.35; P = 0.016; see below)" and "GRNBoost2 ranks last everywhere in our hands, bulk and single cell, whereas BEELINE placed it near GENIE3; we are checking our implementation before making any claim that involves it [PENDING]."
   - New: "GRNBoost2 scores below it in 6 of 7 datasets (mean log2 −0.15; Wilcoxon P = 0.031, sign P = 0.125; the exception is hESC, +0.015; Supplementary Note 13)" and "GRNBoost2 was first reported with a defective early-stopping rule and ranked last everywhere; after the rerun with legacy early stopping its median AUPRC ratio is 1.47, against 1.69 for GENIE3, 1.56 for PIDC, 1.48 for CLR and 1.20 for |r| (Supplementary Table 10)."
   - Why: with n = 7 the exact Wilcoxon P cannot go below 0.0156, and the sign test P is 0.125. The old text called 7 of 7 with P = 0.016 a result; the new count has Wilcoxon P = 0.031 and sign P = 0.125 (both given, no interpretation added). The reviewer should decide whether "score below" is stated as a finding or a descriptive count, and whether the BEELINE seed sensitivity (seeds 1-3: mean -0.157, -0.157, -0.159; against CLR-NS 0 of 7 for the production seed and seed 1, 1 of 7 for seeds 2 and 3; median ratio 1.456-1.485 straddles CLR's 1.479; these runs had no gate) matters for the median ordering. The removed clause "whereas BEELINE placed it near GENIE3" was dropped without replacement.
