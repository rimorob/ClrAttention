## 1. Method as run

Exactly as in the plan. Data: K562 essential pseudobulk half A, 5,671 genes x 707 profiles. K10 = package CLR -> softmax keff 10, cap 50 -> lazy walk 0.5 -> S_9 = (P^9 + P^9')/2. Ten truths (CORUM_all = "raw", noRP, RP, small 3-6, large >= 20, merged, merged noRP, DoRothEA A-B, CollecTRI, KD-response). Paired delete-half gene jackknife, 100 replicates, seed 20261016, `robust_z` stream order with the merged truths appended; three scopes (raw; hub-stratified by pi of the K10 walk; hub-stratified by the raw-MI row mean); per-complex AP on raw-CORUM complexes with >= 10 pairs. Code: `analysis/explore_ideas47/` (`ideas47_lib.R`, `ideas47_idea4.R`, `ideas47_idea7_depth.R`, `ideas47_idea7_score.R`, `ideas47_inflate.R`, `ideas47_summarize.R`). Small CSVs: `docs/explore_ideas47_tables/`; rds caches under `results/explore_ideas47/` (2.3 MB in total, not committed).

**Idea 4 as run.** c_i = Onnela weighted clustering coefficient of the top-10 attention neighbourhood (W = (A + A')/2, wh = W/max W, 1/90 normalisation). Both terms standardised by the mean and SD of their upper-triangle entries (CLR mean 0.5362, SD 0.8790; a3r mean 0.5464, SD 0.6507). Blends B1-B8 as in the plan.

**Idea 7 as run.** Walk Q_s = rownorm((Q_{s-1} P)^r). Depth by the `robust_z` K562 scheme: random profile halves (each knockdown profile its own "lab"), 6 splits from `lab_split()` under `set.seed(20261014)`, both directions, t = 1..20, criterion = AP of S_t against the test half's top-1% CLR pairs. r in {1, 1.1, 1.2, 1.5}.

## 7. Deviations from the plan

1. None in readouts, grids, truths, jackknife or rules. The tables use all ten truths in all three scopes as planned.
2. Idea 7: because the joint selection returned r* = 1, IN-J and IN-J-min duplicate K10 and K10_min (they were scored again through the inflation code, as planned, and agree exactly). The plan's rule applied to IN-J therefore compares K10 with reciprocity; this is reported as run, with the six r > 1 readouts as the informative rows.
3. The `--quick` smoke tests (600 and 400 genes, 10 replicates) read the truths in-sample after the plan commit and are not reported; their outputs are not kept.
4. The `a3r` common-scale standardisation and the definition of c_i were fixed in the plan after one label-free look at the distribution of c (disclosed in the plan).
5. Memory for 5420 peaked at 21.8 GB against 20 GB declared (cap 26 GB, not hit).
